# QuickShot Privacy Policy

Effective 11 November 2026

QuickShot does not collect, store or share any personal data.

## What stays on your Mac

- **Screenshots.** QuickShot captures the screen only when you start a capture. Images go to the clipboard, the editor, your Snapshot Collection, or a folder you choose. They are never uploaded.
- **Screen recordings.** QuickShot records the screen, and the microphone, system audio or camera only when you turn them on, and only while a recording is running. Recordings stay in a temporary folder on your Mac until you copy or save them; one you discard is deleted. They are never uploaded.
- **Recognised text.** Capture Text and Copy Text use Apple's on-device Vision framework. The image and the text never leave your Mac.
- **Settings.** Your shortcuts, save folder and tool styles are stored in the app's preferences on your Mac.

## What QuickShot does not do

- No account or sign-in.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking.
- No third-party SDKs that collect data.

## Network use

- **App Store version:** QuickShot makes no network connections.
- **Direct download version:** QuickShot checks GitHub for signed app updates. That request is an ordinary download of an update file; like any web request it reveals your IP address to GitHub, and it carries no information about you or your screenshots. You can turn automatic updates off in Settings.

## Permissions

- **Screen & System Audio Recording** is required by macOS for any app that captures the screen. System audio is recorded only during a screen recording with System Audio turned on.
- **Microphone** is asked for only when you turn Microphone on for recordings, and is used only while recording.
- **Camera** is asked for only when you turn Camera on for recordings. The camera picture is shown on your screen in a bubble, which the screen recording includes, and only while recording.
- **Files and folders.** QuickShot can write only to the folder you choose and to files you name in a save dialog.
- **Login item.** QuickShot opens at login only if you turn that on.
